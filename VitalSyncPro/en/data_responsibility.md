# Your Data, Your Responsibility

**Application:** VitalSync Pro

**Version:** 1.0

**Last Updated: 30 August 2026

---

# 1. Our Philosophy

VitalSync Pro is built on a simple principle:

**Your data belongs to you.**

The Application is designed to give you full control over your personal health records.

---

# 2. Local Storage by Default

By default, VitalSync Pro stores your health information only on your own device.

The developer does not automatically receive, upload, collect, or store your personal health information.

---

# 3. Ownership of Your Data

You remain the sole owner of all information entered into the Application.

The developer does not claim ownership of your health records or personal information.

---

# 4. Your Responsibilities

You are responsible for:

- Protecting your device.
- Keeping your device secure.
- Maintaining regular backups.
- Protecting your passwords or biometric authentication.
- Ensuring the accuracy of the information you enter.

---

# 5. Data Loss

Because your information is stored on your own device, you are responsible for maintaining backups.

The developer cannot recover information that has been deleted, lost, or damaged due to:

- Device failure.
- Device loss or theft.
- Operating system failures.
- User error.
- Accidental deletion.
- Hardware damage.

---

# 6. Optional Cloud Services

Future versions of VitalSync Pro may include optional cloud backup or synchronization features.

These services will always require your explicit consent before any information leaves your device.

Using the Application does not automatically enable cloud storage.

---

# 7. Data Export

VitalSync Pro may provide tools to export your information.

You are responsible for protecting exported files and preventing unauthorized access.

---

# 8. Data Deletion

You may delete your information at any time.

Deleted information may not be recoverable unless you have created your own backups.

---

# 9. Security Recommendations

To better protect your information we recommend:

- Using a secure screen lock.
- Keeping your operating system updated.
- Encrypting your device whenever possible.
- Creating regular backups.
- Avoiding installation of untrusted software.

---

# 10. Acceptance

By using VitalSync Pro, you acknowledge that you understand and accept your responsibility for protecting, managing, and backing up your personal health information.

---

Thank you for choosing a privacy-focused health management application.

Your data belongs to you.

---

# Current backup and cloud services

VitalSync Pro currently supports optional local backups and optional Google Drive backups. A Google Drive backup is created or restored only when you choose to use that feature and authorize the required Google account access. The developer does not operate a server that automatically receives your personal health records.

---

# Security and authentication

VitalSync Pro can offer an optional app lock using a PIN or password and supported Android biometric authentication. Biometric templates are handled by the Android operating system; the Application does not store fingerprint or facial biometric templates. Security credentials and recovery information are device-local and are excluded from application backups.

---

# Permissions and device features

Depending on the features you choose, the Application may request permissions for notifications, physical activity recognition, location, or related device capabilities. Permissions are requested to provide the selected feature and can be managed through Android settings. Location and activity features are not a medical monitoring or emergency service.
