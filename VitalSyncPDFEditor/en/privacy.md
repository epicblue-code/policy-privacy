# Privacy Policy

## Privacy Policy

VitalSync PDF Editor ("the app", "we", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles files, information, subscriptions, and advertising.

## 1. PDF Files and Local Processing

VitalSync PDF Editor is designed to process PDF files primarily on your device.

Depending on the feature you use, the app may allow you to:

- Create PDF files
- Edit PDF files
- Merge or split PDF files
- Convert PDF files and other supported documents
- Compress PDF files
- Extract or organize PDF pages
- View and save PDF files

Your files are processed locally on your device by the app. The app does not operate a developer-controlled cloud service for uploading or storing your PDF files.

## 2. Access to Files

The app accesses files only when required to provide a feature requested by you.

For example, you may select a PDF, image, or other supported file for processing. The app may also receive a PDF file when you explicitly open or share it with VitalSync PDF Editor from another application.

The app uses the selected or shared file only for the requested operation.

## 3. Locally Stored Information

Some information may be stored locally on your device to provide app functionality and improve your experience. This may include:

- Recently opened file information
- App preferences and display settings
- Locally created signatures
- Subscription-related information required by the app
- Other information necessary for local app functionality

This information is stored locally using the app's local storage/database.

## 4. Signatures

If you create a signature using the app, such as a drawn, text-based, or image-based signature, the signature may be stored locally on your device so that you can manage and reuse it.

The app does not upload your locally created signatures to a developer-controlled server.

## 5. Premium Subscriptions

VitalSync PDF Editor may offer Premium subscriptions through Google Play.

Subscription purchases and payment processing are handled by Google Play and the Google Play Billing system.

The app may store subscription-related information locally on your device to manage Premium access and restore purchases. Google Play remains the relevant platform for processing subscription transactions.

Please refer to Google's applicable privacy policies for information about how Google handles payment and purchase-related data.

## 6. Advertising

The app includes advertising infrastructure and may display advertisements to users who are not entitled to an ad-free Premium experience.

Advertising services are provided through Google Mobile Ads technologies.

Google and advertising partners may process certain information as described in their own privacy policies and in accordance with applicable laws and platform requirements. The types of information processed can depend on factors such as your device, region, consent choices, and the advertising services in use.

For more information, please review Google's privacy information and advertising policies.

## 7. Internet Connection

The app may use an internet connection for features such as:

- Advertising services
- Google Play Billing and subscription-related functionality
- Other services provided by the Android or Google Play platform

PDF editing and processing features are designed to operate locally where supported by the relevant feature.

## 8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your PDF files or locally created signatures.

The app does not provide a developer-controlled cloud service for storing your PDF files.

Third-party services, such as Google Play Billing and Google Mobile Ads, may process information according to their own terms, privacy policies, and applicable platform requirements.

## 9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps in the design of the app to minimize unnecessary handling of user files. Files and locally stored app information remain subject to the security of your device and operating system.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Children's Privacy

VitalSync PDF Editor is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly operate a developer-controlled service for collecting personal information from children through the app.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its features change.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made.

## 12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the application's official Google Play listing or official project pages.
